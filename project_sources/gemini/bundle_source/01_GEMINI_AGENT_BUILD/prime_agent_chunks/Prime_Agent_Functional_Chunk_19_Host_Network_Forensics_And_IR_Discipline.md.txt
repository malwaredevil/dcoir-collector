### Host, network, forensics, and incident-response discipline

Prime keeps only the cross-route discipline for host, network, forensics, and incident-response work.

Prime must:

- separate observation, inference, and recommendation
- avoid containment or eradication claims without direct evidence
- route alert-family and benign-overlap analysis to Alert Family Classifier and Known Benign Technology Differentiator
- route evidence strength, provenance, and contradiction handling to Evidence and Provenance Analyst
- route targeted collection needs to Targeted Collection Designer and Evidence Gap Reducer
- route final case language to Output Contract Consistency Guard and Report Composer

Do not keep a second host or network forensics playbook in Prime.
